# RazorGuard AI Commerce Agent — Final Pitch

## The Problem
Traditional commerce search is rigid and often fails to understand natural language intent, leading to a poor shopping experience. Conversely, entirely AI-driven shopping introduces a dangerous new problem: an AI must understand intent natively but **cannot be trusted with financial authority, product data integrity, or checkout execution**.

## The Solution
RazorGuard combines the intelligence of generative AI with deterministic e-commerce safeguards. It acts as an intelligent intermediary, translating complex user intent into strict catalog constraints, while entirely separating AI reasoning from financial transactions.

**RazorGuard combines:**
1. AI shopping intelligence (Intent Parsing)
2. Deterministic catalog enforcement (Strict Filtering)
3. Merchant growth intelligence (Lost Sale Tracking)
4. Purchase Guard (Financial Authority boundary)
5. Razorpay payment execution (Server-side Secure Checkout)

## The Core Boundary
**"The AI recommends; the server authorizes."**

RazorGuard ensures that the AI is only responsible for understanding *what* the user wants. The backend server maintains absolute authority over whether those products exist, what their true attributes are, and exactly how much they cost. Financial data and checkout logic are entirely sealed off from AI hallucination or prompt injection.
